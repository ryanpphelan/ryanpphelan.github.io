The moment

More dashboards than ever. The same meeting every Monday.

Marketing has its numbers, Sales has its pipeline, Finance has its forecast. Each team has its own list of accounts at risk, and the lists barely overlap. Someone still has to decide what needs action this week, who owns it, and whether last month’s call worked. That someone is usually a person with a spreadsheet and a standing meeting.

Meanwhile the AI pilots pile up, and most of them speed up a step nobody ever defined. AI doesn’t fix a fuzzy decision. It scales whatever you give it, so a fuzzy decision just arrives faster.

What I build

Systems that watch your data, surface what matters, and check whether it worked.

Each system watches your own data, finds what deserves attention, recommends the action and the owner, and then measures whether the action worked. Every recommendation shows the evidence behind it. Nothing acts without the approval you set.

Revenue and marketing intelligence. Which leads, accounts and campaigns need action this week, and why.

Executive intelligence. Commitments, risks and decisions pulled out of mail, meetings and project tools onto one desk.

Operational intelligence. Silent failures and anomalies caught before a customer notices.

Decision systems. The information a recurring decision needs, assembled the same way every time instead of by hand.

Governed AI workflows. AI doing real work inside approval steps you control.

Every system is designed for your business. I don’t install a copy of mine or resell someone else’s platform.

How it starts

With the decision, not the tool.

Before anything is built, we agree on the recurring decision that matters most, who owns it, what good looks like, and where its information lives. It’s the same belief behind the Clarity Gap: AI scales whatever you give it, so what you give it has to be right first.

Three ways to work together

Start where you are.

Intelligence Diagnostic

Three to four weeks, fixed fee, and optional. Interviews with your leaders, a read-only look at the systems behind the decisions that matter, and a prioritized set of opportunities with data readiness, risks and a 90-day roadmap. If you already know the decision, we skip it.

Design and Build

A short design phase sets exactly how the first system works, how it will be tested and what it will take. Then I build one focused capability, pilot it against acceptance tests we agree on in advance, and hand it over or run it.

Run and Improve

I operate the system for you: recurring briefs, exceptions caught, recommendations scored against what actually happened, and improvements every month. You keep the decisions.

How every system is built

Governance isn’t an add-on. It’s how the work is done.

Evidence first. Every recommendation cites the data it came from. If it can’t show its source, it doesn’t show up.

People decide. AI proposes. Rules and people approve. Nothing reaches your customers or changes your systems without an approval step you sign off on.

Measured, not assumed. Whether a signal was right, whether anyone acted on it, and whether the action worked are tracked separately. I don’t claim credit a comparison can’t support.

Built to stop. Every system I deliver includes spend limits, a way to switch it off, and a record of what it did and why.

Yours, and kept separate. Your data, accounts and approval decisions stay yours. Each client’s system runs on accounts and credentials that client controls, separate from every other client’s, and that separation is tested before the system touches live data.

Built, not just discussed

I run my own practice on systems I designed and built.

A chief-of-staff system. It reads my mail, calendar, meetings and project tools and puts what needs my attention on one desk. Every item links to the exact words that created it. It’s in daily use, and its judgment about what deserves attention is still being measured and improved.

A governed content operation. It grades ideas, drafts posts and fact-checks them before anything reaches me. Nothing publishes without my approval.

The Clarity Gap Diagnostic. A multi-stage research system that tests what a company says against what the market and AI systems hear back. It’s in public beta, and every report is reviewed by me before it goes out.

In a conversation I’ll show you how they work and how they perform, including where they’re still improving. Behind them: nearly three decades building and running marketing, CRM and lifecycle systems at enterprise scale, as an operator and as a CEO.

Plainly

What I don’t do.

I don’t sell an off-the-shelf platform and call it a custom intelligence system. I design and build around your business, using the right technology for the job. I don’t promise financial results I haven’t measured. I don’t offer legal, compliance or security certification. Every engagement states what has been tested and what hasn’t.

Who it’s for

CEOs, CMOs, CROs and COOs at established B2B and consumer companies who make recurring decisions with scattered information, and who want a system they can trust, not another dashboard or another pilot.

Fit depends on the decision more than the size of the company: a recurring decision worth improving, a scope we can bound, and someone ready to own it and invest in getting it right.
