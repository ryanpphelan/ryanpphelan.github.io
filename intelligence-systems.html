How a system works

From a business signal to a measured result.

Where it standsEvery step below runs in one of my working systems today. Built for a client, it runs on your data and accounts you control, around the decisions you name.

01 · NoticeSpot the condition

Watches your sources on a schedule. A quiet source that's healthy is reported as quiet; a broken one as broken.

02 · InterpretRead the evidence

Quotes what it found, where it found it, and states what it doesn't know.

03 · RecommendPropose an action

One recommendation, with an owner and a date to check it.

04 · DecideYour people approve

Nothing goes out, changes or closes until a person says yes.

05 · MeasureCheck what happened

Hits, misses and false alarms are scored. Results are credited only against a fair comparison.
