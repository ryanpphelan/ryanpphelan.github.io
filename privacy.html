Privacy Policy

How this site handles your information. Last updated September 12, 2026.

The short version

  • This is the marketing site for The Strongbow Group LLC. It has no accounts, no logins, and no payments.
  • Nothing that tracks you loads until you choose. Decline the cookie banner and analytics and visitor identification stay off.
  • If you accept, I use Google Analytics to understand traffic, and a US business-visitor service (RB2B) that may tell me which business a US visitor is from and the professional profile behind the visit.
  • You can book a conversation through a scheduling tool (OnceHub), which collects your name, email, and meeting details.
  • I do not sell your personal information. You can change your cookie choice anytime under “Cookie settings” in the footer, and you can email me to see, correct, or delete what I hold.

Who I am

The Strongbow Group LLC (“I,” “me,” or “the Strongbow Group”) operates ryanphelan.com and is responsible for the personal data described in this policy. If you have any questions, email me at ryanp@ryanphelan.com.

Information I collect

Information you give me directly. When you book a conversation through the scheduling tool on the Contact page, I receive your name, email address, the time you select, and anything you type into the booking form. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.

Information collected automatically, only after you accept. If you accept the cookie banner, Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager set cookies and collect usage data: the pages you view, an approximate location derived from your IP address, your device and browser type, and how you move through the site. Your consent choice itself is stored in your browser so the site remembers it. None of this loads if you decline.

Business visitor identification, only after you accept, US visitors only. If you accept the cookie banner and you are visiting from the United States, a third-party service called RB2B may identify the business and professional profile associated with your visit, which can include a name, job title, employer, company domain, and a business email address, drawn from LinkedIn and other professional data sources. RB2B performs this person-level identification only for visitors it verifies as US-based. According to RB2B, it is designed to exclude the personal data of EU and UK residents and does not identify EU or UK individuals at the person level; for non-US visitors, at most the company, not the individual, may be identified. Because I gate this service behind the cookie banner, it does not run at all unless you accept.

How I use information

I use the information above to respond to your inquiry and prepare for a conversation you request, to understand how the site is used so I can improve it, to reach out to business visitors identified through RB2B about relevant consulting services, and to keep the site secure and working. I do not use this information to make automated decisions that produce legal effects about you, and I do not build advertising profiles about you for third parties.

Legal bases, for EU and UK visitors

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, I rely on your consent for analytics cookies and for RB2B visitor identification, which you can withdraw at any time; on steps taken at your request before any engagement, when you book or ask about working together; and on my legitimate interests in responding to your inquiries, running and securing the site, and modest business-to-business development, balanced against your rights.

Cookies and your choices

The site uses Google Consent Mode. Before you choose, all analytics and marketing storage is denied by default and nothing that tracks you loads. The banner lets you Accept or Decline: Accept turns on analytics and RB2B, Decline keeps them off. You can change your mind at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer. The main cookies are Google Analytics cookies (named _ga and _ga_<id>), set only after you accept. Your consent choice is stored in your browser’s local storage and is not shared with anyone. You can also control cookies through your browser settings.

Who I share information with

I do not sell your personal information. I share it only with the service providers that make the site work, each acting on my behalf or under its own terms: Google for analytics, OnceHub for scheduling, and RB2B for US business-visitor identification. I may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect my rights or the safety of others.

Where your information is processed

I operate from the United States, and the providers above process data in the US and potentially in other countries. If you are in the EU, the UK, or another region with data-transfer rules, your information may be transferred to and processed in the US. Where required, those transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the providers’ standard contractual clauses.

How long I keep it

I keep personal data only as long as I need it: scheduling and contact information for as long as I need to correspond with you and for a reasonable period afterward, analytics data according to Google Analytics’ retention settings, and visitor-identification data for as long as it is useful for business development or until you ask me to delete it.

The Clarity Gap Diagnostic

The diagnostic at clarity.ryanphelan.com collects more than the rest of this site, so it gets its own section. It runs on a subdomain of this site and is covered by this policy rather than a separate one.

What you give me. Your company’s website address, so I know what to look at. Your name, work email address, job title, functional area, your relationship to the company, and how involved you are in its strategy. I ask for the last two because they change how much weight your answers carry, not because they change the analysis.

Your answers to the interview questions. These describe your company’s positioning, your customers, your competitors, and in some cases what is not working. Treat them as you would treat anything you would say in a first meeting with a consultant.

What I collect automatically. A device identifier and your IP address, used to limit how many diagnostics can be started from one place. Page views through Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and does not follow you to other sites. Web addresses are stripped of anything identifying before they are recorded.

What I collect about your company. The diagnostic reads your company’s public website, public information about your competitors, and public third-party material about your company: reviews, coverage, directory listings, conference programs. All of it is material already published to the open internet.

Some of that material names other people, such as a reviewer or an executive quoted in an article. Those people did not ask to be part of your diagnostic. I use what they published only to establish how the market describes your company, I do not build profiles of them, and I do not include material about them in anything I deliver to you.

Who processes it. Running the diagnostic means passing information through services I do not own. Each one sees only what it needs. The analysis runs through two AI models rather than one, from Anthropic and from OpenAI, so two independent readings can be compared instead of trusting a single one.

How long I keep it. I keep your interview answers, the diagnosis, and the research material gathered about your company for 24 months, because the analysis is only useful if I can refer back to it when we speak. Your report is generated from that material when you open it rather than stored as a file, and its link can be switched off at any time. I record when your report is first opened and how many times it has been opened, so I know it reached you. I do not record what you read, how long you spent, or where you were. That record is deleted with the rest of your diagnostic material.

Verification emails carry a single-use link. I never store the link itself, only a one-way fingerprint of it, so it cannot be recovered from my records even by me.

A human reads it. At present I read every report before it is delivered. That is deliberate and it is how the method is being calibrated. It also means a real person sees your answers. If that is not something you want, do not start a diagnostic.

Your choices. Email ryanp@ryanphelan.com to see, correct, delete, or get a copy of anything the diagnostic holds about you. You can ask me to delete any of it at any time and I will delete what I hold. If you have downloaded or forwarded a copy of your report, that copy is yours and is outside anything I can reach.

Your rights

EU and UK visitors also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. To exercise any of these, email me at ryanp@ryanphelan.com and I will respond. To opt out of RB2B specifically, decline the cookie banner, or accept and then change your choice under “Cookie settings.”

Security

I take reasonable measures to protect personal data, but no website or method of transmission is perfectly secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This site is intended for a business audience and is not directed to children under 16. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy
