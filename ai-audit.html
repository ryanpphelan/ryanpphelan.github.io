VOICES 2026

The Monday Morning AI Audit

Pick one AI use on your team. Not all of them. One. Answer these five questions. It takes about fifteen minutes.

1. ACCESS What can it access?

A good answer: A written list of the data, accounts and systems it can read or change, and read-only wherever that is enough.

A warning sign: “Whatever the vendor set up.”

2. CLAIM What is it allowed to claim, and on what evidence?

A good answer: It says where each fact came from, and anything it cannot source is labeled unknown, not filled in.

A warning sign: Numbers and statements nobody can trace.

3. DECIDE What does it decide alone?

A good answer: A short list of decisions it makes without a person, every one of them reversible.

A warning sign: Nobody on the team can list them.

4. APPROVE Who approves its work?

A good answer: A named person reviews the full output before it reaches a customer or the public.

A warning sign: Approval happens on a summary, or “the tool handles that.”

5. OWN Whose name is on the outcome?

A good answer: One name.

A warning sign: A committee, a vendor, or silence.

If question five does not have one name, that is your governance gap. You found it before it found you.

You can delegate the work. You cannot delegate the accountability.
