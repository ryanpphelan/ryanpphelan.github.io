Ways to Work Together

Every engagement runs on the same method.

What changes is how far we take it — and how much of the work you want in my hands.

The Clarity Sprint

The moment. You can feel it before you can name it. Growth that used to come easily now takes more effort. Your team is busy, hitting its activity targets, and somehow the market still doesn’t quite get you. Every leader describes the company a little differently. You look at your own website and think, “that’s not really us anymore.” Nothing is on fire. But something is off.

What it is. A focused, 30-day engagement — an outside read on your Clarity Gap. I run stakeholder conversations across leadership and the teams closest to your customers, review how you actually show up in the market, and work through your own numbers and plans.

What you get. Not a report you file. A one-page leadership summary your team reads together; a central diagnosis with the three findings that matter most; a Growth Opportunity Map that sequences the moves; and a 30/60/90-day roadmap where every item carries an owner, a success metric, and a decision date. Plus an honest scope section — what I examined, what I didn’t, and what’s still directional.

How I work. I come in structured, not with opinions. Everything runs through the six-part method, so you don’t leave with forty fixes — you leave with the few that move the business, in order, and a working session to align the team behind them.

Who it’s for. CEOs, founders, and CMOs heading into a next stage: a launch, a raise, an acquisition, or a reset.

Fractional CMO & Market Strategy

The moment. You need senior marketing judgment in the room — not another agency, and not a full-time CMO you’re not ready to hire. Someone who’s carried the number, sat at the table, and can set direction and hold it.

What it is. An ongoing partnership. I act as your senior marketing leader — setting strategy, prioritizing, and keeping the company aligned as products, markets, and teams keep changing.

How I work. Alignment isn’t a one-time event. Markets move, you acquire, you launch, a competitor shifts. I stay close enough to protect the clarity we build and evolve it as you grow — and pull in specialists from my network when a push needs them.

What you get. Experienced marketing leadership measured by clarity and consistency, not just output — and a steadier hand than a rotating bench of vendors.

Who it’s for. Growth-stage teams with a marketing function but a gap at the top.

Hands-On Execution

The moment. You don’t need another strategy deck. You’ve got strategy. You need someone senior to actually do the work — and do it right.

What it is. Scoped, hands-on work: positioning and messaging, website strategy, email and lifecycle, CRM and martech including platform migrations, AI strategy and governance.

How I work. You hire me, I do the work — not a pitch from me followed by delivery from juniors you’ve never met. When the work needs a specialist I’m not, I bring in a proven one from my network. The senior person stays on the job from diagnosis through build.

What you get. The work done by the person you actually hired, grounded in the same method so execution reinforces your position instead of drifting from it.

Who it’s for. Teams that value knowing exactly who’s in the weeds with them.

You get me — and the right people when you need them

Everything starts and ends with me. I’m your senior point of accountability from the first conversation to the last deliverable — never a pitch followed by a hand-off to juniors. And when the work needs a specialist I’m not, I bring in a proven one from nearly three decades of relationships, matched to your problem rather than pulled off a bench.

One senior operator, plus access to exactly the right people. The Strongbow Group is the shingle. The work is mine.
